market-risk-var-es

Market risk VaR and Expected Shortfall

One-day Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (ES) for a small constant-weight portfolio, with the two standard coverage backtests: Kupiec unconditional coverage, and Christoffersen independence and conditional coverage. Historical and parametric (variance-covariance) estimates are both included. The numbers come from a recorded file of public ETF prices.

market-risk-streaming-lake

Market risk streaming lake

A small batch pipeline for synthetic market-risk reporting. A producer writes factor, position, and P&L events to a bus. A consumer lands them in a bronze / silver / gold Parquet lake, scores book limits, and prints a short explanation of one breach. The default run uses an in-process bus, a local directory as the lake, and a fixed text template. Apache Kafka, Azure Data Lake Storage Gen2, and an OpenAI-compatible model are optional and stay off unless turned on.

market-risk-streaming-lake on GitHub

market-risk-pnl-attribution

Market risk P&L attribution

One session of linear P&L for a small snapshot book, split into the market move on the opening quantity and the P&L from a quantity change, then a stated-beta factor split of the market piece. The book, the prices, and the factor returns are synthetic.
